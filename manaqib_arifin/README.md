# مناقب العارفین — اردو ترجمہ (Manaqib al-'Arifin — Urdu Translation)

## یہ کتاب کیا ہے / What this book is

**مناقب العارفین** (*Manaqib al-'Arifin*, "The Virtues of the Gnostics") is a classical
Persian Sufi hagiography written in the 14th century (completed c. 1353–1362 CE) by
**Shams al-Din Ahmad Aflaki al-'Arifi**, a disciple within the Mevlevi (Mawlawi) order.
It narrates the lives and spiritual feats of Jalal al-Din Rumi, his father Baha al-Din
Walad ("Sultan al-'Ulama"), his teacher Burhan al-Din Muhaqqiq, his companion Shams-i
Tabrizi, his successors Salah al-Din Zarkub and Husam al-Din Chalabi, his son Sultan
Walad, and later descendants of the Mevlevi lineage.

The specific edition used as the source for this translation is not Aflaki's original
Persian text directly, but a later **abridged compilation** of it — a lithographed
Persian/Urdu print titled *Sawanih-i 'Umri Hazrat Maulana Rum, musamma ba Manaqib
al-'Arifin*, prepared by **Maulana Muhammad Qamar al-Din**, printed at Matba
Sitara-i-Hind, Agra, for the benefit of readers connected to Madrasa Sa'adat al-Islam,
Ajmer Sharif. The scanned copy translated here bears library markings from the Maulana
Azad Library, Aligarh Muslim University, and was sourced from a public digitization
hosted on archive.org. Both Aflaki's original work and this particular old lithograph
edition are historical public-domain texts.

## اس ترجمے کا طریقہ کار / How this translation was made

This Urdu translation was produced by an AI system (Claude), working directly from
scanned page images of the lithograph print — **not** from a machine-readable text
layer. The source is hand-lithographed **Nastaliq calligraphy**, a script style that
standard OCR tools cannot reliably read, so each of the book's 408 pages was read
visually, page by page, and translated into Urdu. The output PDF preserves the
original book's pagination: each output page corresponds to one source page, and the
original printed page number (where legible in the scan) is reproduced at the top of
the corresponding page.
